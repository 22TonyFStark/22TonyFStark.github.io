DragonMoCap

Monocular motion capture for diverse dragon skeletons

DragonMoCap targets heterogeneous dragon characters in games and film. Unlike methods tied to a fixed human-body template, it takes the target skeleton itself as input and provides a unified solution for dragon rigs with different joint counts, topologies, and naming conventions.

  • Data and model: Built a UE5 dataset containing 14 professionally rigged dragon characters and approximately 600,000 animation frames. The model extracts visual features with DINOv2, combines CLIP semantics from bone names with skeletal topology to construct joint queries, and uses a shared Transformer to predict root motion and local joint rotations.
  • Core innovation: Introduces skeleton-query-based variable-topology decoding and non-parametric retrieval, allowing the model to adapt to previously unseen character rigs without fine-tuning.
  • Performance: Achieves 13.85 cm MPJPE and 8.63 cm PA-MPJPE on six unseen skeletons while running at approximately 30 FPS on a single NVIDIA L20 GPU, providing a general and economical motion-capture solution for heterogeneous non-human character animation.

LifeSimWorld

A unified video world model for understanding and generating open-ended life simulation

A video world model for everyday life interactions. Unlike existing systems centered on navigation, combat, or short-horizon control, LifeSimWorld focuses on long-duration character–environment and character–character interactions such as sitting, eating, conversation, furniture use, and transitions between indoor and outdoor spaces.

  • Data system: Built a UE4SS-based automated capture and post-processing pipeline that synchronizes game video, camera trajectories, character states, and behavioral semantics, followed by temporal calibration, viewpoint filtering, person detection, black-border removal, and duplicate-frame cleaning. Beyond structured life-simulation data, collected and curated approximately 200 hours of high-quality AAA game footage to expand the diversity of environments, character behavior, and visual dynamics.
  • Unified model: Designed and implemented a Qwen- and Wan2.2-based architecture that unifies video understanding and generation. Qwen models scene semantics, character state, and high-level behavioral instructions, while Wan2.2 learns visual dynamics and generates action-conditioned future video, connecting current-world understanding with future-state prediction and synthesis. Model training and system validation are in progress.
  • Core value: Connects structured game state, internet-scale high-quality video, and multimodal understanding–generation models into a complete data-to-model loop, providing a unified foundation for long-horizon controllable video generation, interactive world simulation, and embodied-intelligence training.

Reasoning-Structured Videos

A hierarchical diagnostic benchmark for compositional consistency in video world models

A structured evaluation framework for long-horizon controllable video generation. Beyond perceptual metrics such as FVD, LPIPS, and PSNR, it tests whether a model preserves the same underlying world across composed actions and multiple equivalent paths, decomposing failures into interpretable dimensions of realism, internal consistency, relation type, and temporal horizon.

  • Problem formulation: Models consistency in video world models as a cross-path compositional reasoning problem. Inverse, Loop, and Equivalence relations respectively diagnose action cancellation, closed-loop revisitation, and agreement between distinct paths that reach the same state.
  • Data system: Builds a deterministic UE5 capture pipeline that organizes trajectories as rooted graphs with shared initial states, paired discrete action sequences, and continuous ground-truth camera poses. Geometric constraints, pose verification, and two-stage pixel validation ensure every algebraic relation holds exactly in the real data.
  • Hierarchical evaluation: Introduces complementary GT-anchor and Self-Consistency protocols to separate real-world fidelity from consistency under a model's own control semantics. The latter is invariant to model-specific action scales and produces relation- and horizon-resolved capability profiles across heterogeneous control interfaces.
  • Theory and validation: Shows from dynamical identifiability that one-step and even full-history prediction cannot identify the quotient-state structure induced by cross-path equivalence, while algebraically meaningful relations become exponentially rare in random data as the horizon grows. Experiments on Chunk-AR, Matrix-Game 2.0, and Infinite-World reveal model-specific relation difficulty and GT/SC gaps hidden by aggregate metrics.

Story-to-Motion

Synthesizing Infinite and Controllable Character Animation from Long Text

Zhongfei Qing, Zhongang Cai, Zhitao Yang, and Lei Yang

A language-driven system for generating controllable, infinitely long character motions and trajectories from narrative text. It combines an LLM-based motion scheduler, semantic motion retrieval, and a progressive mask transformer to produce coherent animation across long stories.

  • Problem formulation: Unified long-horizon language understanding, semantic motion composition, trajectory tracking, and transition synthesis in a single generation problem rather than isolated subtasks.
  • Core algorithm: Designed an LLM-based hierarchical motion scheduler, semantic-and-trajectory constrained motion matching, and a progressive masked-attention Transformer that achieved state-of-the-art results on motion inbetweening.
  • Physical correction: Derived SMPL inverse kinematics with swing-twist decomposition and AMASS-trained data-driven refinement to jointly address terrain adaptation, penetration, floating, and foot sliding.

SynBody

Synthetic Dataset with Layered Human Models for 3D Human Perception and Modeling

A large-scale synthetic human dataset built with UE5 for 3D human perception. I designed the camera and character placement system, including visibility, occlusion, and scene-aware layout planning.

  • Dataset system: Constructed a high-dimensional procedural generation space over scenes, identities, garments, motions, lighting, and viewpoints, producing layered ground truth for multiple 3D perception tasks.
  • Core algorithm: Designed the paper's character placement and camera planning modules as a constrained search problem balancing visibility, occlusion, framing quality, and distribution diversity.
  • Robustness: Built scalable rejection sampling and geometric validation for human-scene collision, action feasibility, navigation paths, and camera-to-subject constraints.

QQ Speed AI Pet Motion

Cross-skeleton animation retargeting and secondary motion

Built dance animation for an interactive in-game pet, improving cross-skeleton retargeting with position-based dynamics and adding physically inspired feather and tail motion.

  • Retargeting challenge: Solved severe topology and proportion mismatch between source humanoid motion and a highly non-human parrot skeleton in a shipped real-time product.
  • Constraint solver: Added a position-based dynamics post-process over Unreal Engine retargeting to enforce collision, pose, and continuity constraints without destroying dance rhythm.
  • Secondary dynamics: Constructed spring-mass animation for feathers and tail with stable parameterization under fast motion, producing responsive yet controllable physical detail.

Multi-person motion reconstruction for rapid game prototyping
